Qwen Image Editor

Edit and create images with Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo, entirely on this PC.

What happens next:
- This installer adds the editor and a Start menu shortcut (about 50 MB).
- At the end it installs the editor's components (about 2 GB to download, a few minutes).
- The editor then runs from the tray (by the clock) and opens in your browser and walks you through a one-time setup: it checks your PC, asks you to accept the model's license, and downloads the model (about 17 GB). If you already have the models in ComfyUI, it can use those instead.

Requirements: an NVIDIA graphics card with 12 GB of memory or more, an NVIDIA driver from 2025 or later (version 580+), 32 GB of RAM recommended, and about 25 GB of free disk.

The model, Qwen-Image-2.1, is released under the Qwen Research License Agreement: research and other non-commercial use only. It is not included in this installer.
